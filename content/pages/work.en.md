The repositories under [github.com/boggioMichael](https://github.com/boggioMichael) are the portfolio: real code, with CI, tests and honest evidence documents.

## What a working relationship looks like

- **Architecture and review.** A second brain on a design that has to survive contact with hardware, or a reading of a codebase before an investment or a hire.
- **Prototyping.** A working prototype in weeks, with a written account of what it proves and what it does not.
- **Debugging.** Time-boxed, with a report at the end whether or not the bug was found.
- **Low-level Android/Linux work.** Scoped engagements on platform code.
- **Technical consulting.** Hourly or per project.
- **Unusual engineering problems.** The ones that do not fit a job description.

Confidential prior work stays confidential; nothing from previous employers is described, shown or reused.
