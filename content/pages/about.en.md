Datta is the umbrella under which Michael Boggio builds things that do not fit neatly into one discipline.

The pattern is easier to see than to name. An engineer who spent roughly six years inside Android and Linux at the level where the operating system meets the hardware — C and C++, ARM, reverse engineering, the kind of production debugging where the bug is three layers below the one you were asked to look at — and who, in the same years, kept playing the piano seriously enough to record Bach's *Well-Tempered Clavier* prelude by prelude. A writer who works in Hebrew, English and Spanish, sometimes in the same week. A founder who would rather make one pistachio ice cream worth selling than ten flavours that are fine.

Datta exists because those things were never separate. The same habit runs through all of them: take a question seriously, build the smallest real thing that answers it, measure it honestly, write down what was learned, and keep the result where other people can see it.

## What the studio does

- **Software and AI research.** [[projects/maplesyrup]] reads a running game through pixels alone and turns them into structured, confidence-scored state; [[projects/syrup-universal]] asks whether a companion can learn *any* game alongside its player. [[lab/syrup]] is the generic engine underneath.
- **Products.** [[projects/doggystyle]], a conversational service for dog owners, exists end to end as a working local build with a recorded walkthrough. [[projects/rasa]] is a pistachio gelato in product development, with its first sale on the books.
- **Music.** Performances, a children's album in production ([[music/neshikot-batzintzenet]]) and a recording identity in the making ([[music/limos]]).
- **Writing and research.** Essays, poetry, lyrics and fiction, plus a research notebook where speculative models are labelled as speculative — for instance [[research/the-limit-of-experience]], the philosophical model behind the screenplay [[projects/a-limit]].

## Background

Six years of embedded and platform work on Android/AOSP and embedded Linux in C and C++, including system-level debugging, reverse engineering and mentoring; Rust and TypeScript in the current projects. Pianist. Based in Israel.

Nothing on this site is a claim about what Datta will be. It is a record of what exists, what is being built, and what is still a question. Where a number appears, it was measured; where something is a vision, it says so.
