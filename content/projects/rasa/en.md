## Why pistachio

Pistachio is the most expensive common flavour in the case and the one most often faked: green colour, almond paste, a nut you can barely taste. It is also the flavour with the most room between "fine" and "exceptional". Rasa takes the narrow road — one flavour, built from the nut itself, and rebuilt until there is nothing left to argue about.

## The product

A pistachio gelato in two formulations, developed in parallel:

- **Milk-based** — the classic Italian structure: milk, a little cream, sugars, pistachio paste. Dense, cold, slow to melt.
- **Water-based** — no dairy; the nut carries the fat. Cleaner on the palate, closer to the nut, harder to get right.

The first sale was the water-based base: 2.5 litres, made to order, in September 2026.

## The lab

Rasa is run like a lab, not like a shop. Each batch changes one thing. Each batch is tasted against the previous one and written up — texture, meltdown, how the nut comes through, what the cost per litre did. The equipment today is a countertop machine; the next step is a professional micro-batch processor, which is what the current ask is for.

## Iterations

What the batches are converging on:

- **Pistachio percentage** — enough nut to taste like pistachio and nothing else, without a paste so heavy the texture turns waxy.
- **Sugars** — the balance between sweetness, softness in the freezer and a clean finish.
- **Fat** — in the milk base, from dairy; in the water base, entirely from the nut.
- **Overrun** — how much air the base holds, which sets the difference between a spoon that glides and one that scrapes.
- **Serving temperature** — the window in which the nut is loudest.

The written protocol for tasting and texture is the next thing to formalise, so that every batch is judged the same way.

## Economics

The plan is deliberately small. Six months of formulation work are costed month by month through February 2027, including raw materials, energy and the equipment step. Revenue so far is one sale; the point of the next six months is not revenue but a repeatable product — three consecutive batches within spec — after which the packaging and the first real production decision follow.

## Roadmap

1. Written tasting and texture protocol, applied to every batch.
2. Professional micro-batch processor in the lab.
3. Repeatable product: three consecutive batches within spec.
4. Packaging and label — minimal, a single flavour, nothing that shouts.
5. A first production decision, driven by what the batches and the customers say.
