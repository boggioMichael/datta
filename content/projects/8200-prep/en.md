## What it is

A Hebrew, phone-first practice app for high-school students (grades 11–12) heading into the screening for Unit 8200 and similar technological units. The screening has stages, so the app has stages: psychotechnic reasoning (shapes, analogies, instructions, number series, word problems), learning aptitude (artificial languages, attention, working memory), an intro to programming in Python, a personality questionnaire with situational-judgement items, interview and group-dynamics preparation, and English.

The difference from a question bank is the person behind it. Every student's accuracy per topic, time per question, streak and personality profile sit on a dashboard, and the mentor decides where the next session goes.

## How it works

- **Free** — up to 20 questions a day across every topic, with explanations.
- **PRO** — the full bank, timed simulations, automatic re-drilling of mistakes, personality profile.
- **Mentoring** — PRO plus one-to-one sessions and interview simulations.

Surprise quizzes arrive as notifications: five questions, two minutes. Speed is built, not memorised.
