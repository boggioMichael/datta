## The idea

Most classical recordings on the internet are about the pianist. LIMOS is an attempt to make recordings that are about the piano — close, warm, slightly imperfect in the way a good room is, with the kind of microphone and vinyl aesthetic that makes you lean in rather than scroll past. The performer stays in the background; the visual identity may be a pair of hands, or nothing at all.

## What exists

A name, a direction, and the technical questions above. The recordings that exist today live under [[music/performances]] — the Well-Tempered Clavier series — and are the proof that the playing is there. LIMOS is the question of how it should be *heard*.

## What comes next

A recording chain, a room, and a first set of pieces. When the first LIMOS recording is released it will be linked here, and not before.
