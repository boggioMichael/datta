## Concept

A jar of kisses: the title song's image is a child collecting kisses in a jar and letting them out with a long breath. The album takes childhood seriously — endings, fear, sweetness, bitterness, a cardboard palace, red shoes that only want to run — and sets Noa Horev's lyrics for real instruments: piano, a string quartet, a flute, and a voice chosen for each song rather than one singer for all twelve.

The songs were written roughly a decade ago, with Michael's father, Dario Boggio. Early sketches and a first recording exist; the album is now going into the studio for the first time.

## The songs

Twelve songs. Eight have titles on the page; the rest are listed as they are confirmed. Lyrics are not published here until the recordings are — the song pages will link to them when they are ready.

## Where it stands

- **Written:** the full album, with arrangement notes from the first sketches ("more complication in the harmonies — dissonances, consonances").
- **Planned:** an October 2026 studio session in Tel Aviv — three songs with a string quartet, winds and a new singer.
- **In search of:** a producer for a one-song pilot, self-funded, and illustrators for the visual world of the album.
- **Estimated:** the whole production, everything included, at roughly ₪1M; grants and sponsorship are being pursued milestone by milestone.
