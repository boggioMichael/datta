## The project

Bach's *Well-Tempered Clavier*, Book I: a prelude and a fugue in every key, twenty-four pairs, forty-eight pieces. The project is to record all of it — but to release each piece the moment it is finished, in whatever order it is ready, rather than hold the recordings back for a complete set. The result is a series that grows in public, piece by piece, with the dates on it.

## Published so far

- Prelude No. 2 in C minor, BWV 847

- Prelude No. 9 in E major, BWV 854

The first four preludes are gathered in one video; the Prelude No. 8 and the Fugue No. 9 have videos of their own. Every finished piece is also cut into Shorts and Reels.

## How it is made

One piece at a time. A piece is recorded until there is a take that holds up, edited as little as possible, and published with its catalogue number in the title so that the series can be followed in order later even though it is released out of order now. The order of release follows the playing, not the catalogue: whichever prelude or fugue is ready goes out.

## Where it goes

The remaining forty-two pieces, and then a single playlist in catalogue order. How the finished book should *sound* — the recording chain, the room, the identity of the recordings rather than the performer — is the question [[music/limos]] exists to answer. The idea of printing the scores with their structure in colour lives in [[projects/classical-coloring]].
