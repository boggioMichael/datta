## The Well-Tempered Clavier, prelude by prelude

The long project is Bach's *Well-Tempered Clavier*, Book I — forty-eight preludes and fugues, recorded one at a time and posted as each is finished rather than held back for a complete set. Published so far: the preludes in C major, C minor, E minor and E major; the Prelude No. 8 in E-flat minor; the Fugue No. 9 in E major. The order follows what is ready, not the catalogue.
